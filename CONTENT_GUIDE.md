# AI Adventure Lab Content Guide

This guide keeps new content aligned with the current playable paths.

## High School Student Standard

High School content should feel realistic for grades 9-12. Scenarios should be clear, practical, and age-appropriate without becoming elementary or overly adult.

Good scenario areas:

- essays, projects, presentations, and class discussions
- tutoring, studying, test prep, and learning without cheating
- school AI-use rules, rubrics, and teacher expectations
- clubs, sports, volunteering, scholarships, college/career prep, and part-time jobs
- group chats, social media, screenshots, private messages, and classmate privacy
- current events, science claims, statistics, quotes, and source checking
- bias in recommendations, clubs, hiring, discipline, scholarships, access, and visibility

Avoid:

- content that feels too young for high school
- adult-only legal, workplace, finance, or parenting scenarios
- college-level research complexity unless simplified
- medical, legal, or financial advice that sounds definitive
- prompts that encourage cheating, copying final answers, or bypassing teachers

Core habits to reinforce:

- ask for the assignment rules, rubric, goal, and what the student already tried
- use AI for explanation, feedback, practice, and planning instead of final answers to copy
- check source title, author or organization, date, publication, link/DOI, and what the source actually supports
- summarize or anonymize private details before asking AI for help
- watch for confident answers that do not provide evidence
- choose fair criteria instead of shortcuts like confidence, popularity, device access, or background

## Everyday User Standard

Everyday User content should focus on practical, common situations where AI can help but should not replace verification or judgment.

Current playable categories:

- News and Viral Claims
- Scams and Suspicious Messages

Core habits to reinforce:

- pause before trusting, sharing, clicking, or replying
- verify through original sources or official channels
- check dates, context, author/organization, source quality, and independent confirmation
- watch for pressure, urgency, suspicious links, requests for codes, and requests for payment
- avoid sharing private details unless they are necessary and safe

Possible future Everyday User categories:

- Health Caution
- Shopping Research
- Family And Personal Privacy
- Everyday Finance Caution
- Travel And Local Planning

Possible future audience tracks:

- Job Seeker
- Small Business Owner
- Workplace User
